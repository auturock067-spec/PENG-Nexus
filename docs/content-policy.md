# Content and supplier data policy

This project is an **educational reference prototype**, not a marketplace with verified vendors.

1. Use original explanatory writing; do not scrape or copy supplier catalogs, photos, reviews, certificates or trademarks without permission.
2. Describe **generic product types** rather than pretending to have sellable stock. Never invent prices, factories, certificates, case studies, ratings or testimonials.
3. For technical data, keep source, edition, applicable market, reviewer and reviewed_at metadata in the future knowledge record. Independently verify safety and standards claims before live publication.
4. Separate `supplier_declared`, `public_record_checked`, and `independent_inspection`. A registration record is not proof of operating capacity.
5. Supplier onboarding requires explicit authorization to publish identity, logos, photos and claims; allow corrections and removals.
6. Until a lawful business workflow and privacy policy exist, do not collect RFQs, emails, phone numbers, payments or personal accounts.
7. No tobacco/nicotine category in the first phase, pending jurisdiction-specific compliance review.
8. Before removing noindex: human content review, mobile accessibility, source attribution, legal policies, site-specific contact channel and functional tests.
